Grounded, not hyped

The science beneath the poetry

Each element maps to peer-reviewed work. This is grounding, not a claim of clinical benefit.

Witnessing

The quiet mind

Watching thoughts pass, rather than being carried by them, tracks with reduced activity in the default mode network, the brain system tied to self-referential thought and mind-wandering.

Brewer et al., PNAS 2011; Lutz et al., TiCS 2008
Rootedness

The body and the two selves

Attending to inner bodily signals strengthens the insula and interoceptive network and supports a shift from the narrative self toward the present, experiential self.

Farb et al. 2015; Khalsa et al. 2018; Mehling MAIA 2012
Spaciousness

Awe and the small self

Awe, a response to vastness that requires a shift in one's mental frame, reliably produces a felt shrinking of the self and greater connection, and is linked to wellbeing.

Keltner & Haidt 2003; Piff et al. 2015; Yaden et al. 2017
Meaning

Coherence, purpose, significance

A felt sense of meaning has three facets that can be gently supported without any clinical claim: life makes sense, has direction, and matters.

Martela & Steger, J. Positive Psychology 2016
The Resonance

Breath and calm

Slow breathing near six breaths per minute supports heart-rate-variability coherence and a calmer state, which is why the orb paces you there in Stillness.

Lehrer & Gevirtz 2014; Zaccaro et al. 2018
The Ground

Why we stay gentle

Contemplative practice can bring difficult experiences, and studies find these are common. So we favor gentle, active, connected practice and build in off-ramps.

Lindahl & Britton, PLOS ONE 2017; Britton et al. 2021
The honest mirror

Ground rules

What this is, and is not

  • This is a space for self awareness and inner health. It is not medical care, mental-health treatment, therapy, or a substitute for any of them.
  • It never diagnoses, scores illness, or predicts outcomes. It offers reflections, and you decide what they mean.
  • It is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis or may be unsafe, contact local emergency services or a crisis line, and reach a trusted person or professional.
  • Contemplative practice can surface strong emotions or memories. If you have a history of trauma, psychosis, dissociation, or a severe mental-health condition, please practice only with professional guidance.
  • It is not a guru. It is a mirror. The aim is for you to need it less over time.
  • Your inner life is yours. Data stays on your device by default, you own it, and it is never sold or used for advertising.
